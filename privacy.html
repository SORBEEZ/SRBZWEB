Privacy notes.

How this educational website demo handles information.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This privacy notice describes the Sorbeez student project website as currently built. It is an educational demonstration, not a live store or a substitute for a privacy notice reviewed for a production business.

Information you enter

The ZIP code, newsletter, and sponsor forms on this site are visual demonstrations only. Their entries are not transmitted to Sorbeez or stored by the site. Submitting a form only displays an on-page message in your browser.

Shopping bag storage

If you add a flavor, the demo saves the flavor identifier and quantity in your browser’s local storage so the count can persist between pages and visits on the same browser. This is not an order and does not include payment or checkout details. Use “Clear bag” or clear your browser’s site data to remove the selection.

Third-party resources

This website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address. Review Google’s privacy information for details about its handling of requests.

Children and families

This project site is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into a demo form.

Changes and questions

If this project becomes a real service, this notice should be updated to reflect its actual data practices and reviewed by an appropriate adult or legal professional. The site currently has no functioning data-submission service.

Educational project note: This sample notice is general information, not legal advice.
