ideas with a timeline

The goals we’re working toward

Each target depends on product development, supplier availability, appropriate testing, and the project’s ability to move forward.

♻

environmental

Keep packaging together

Target Q4 2027

Explore stay-attached pouch caps

Our proposed packaging goal is to use tethered caps across every squeeze-pouch production run by Q4 2027. Keeping a cap attached to its pouch is intended to help prevent loose-cap litter; it doesn’t eliminate litter or guarantee recyclability. We would first need compatible packaging, supplier capacity, testing, and production agreements.

🍑

environmental

Make room for imperfect fruit

Target FY 2027

Source more cosmetically imperfect produce

By the end of FY 2027, we aim to secure supplier agreements for a majority of our fruit-purée volume to come from cosmetically imperfect produce. Fruit that doesn’t meet appearance standards may still be suitable as an ingredient, subject to food-safety and quality requirements. No sourcing agreements are in place today.

♡

social

Care for customers and our team

Throughout 2027

Explore a low-sugar, vitamin-inspired product concept

If product development moves forward, our goal is to explore a low-sugar recipe fortified with Hiya Multivitamins throughout 2027, alongside age-appropriate cap hardware designed with choking-risk reduction in mind. Neither the recipe nor ingredients, Hiya supplier relationship, nutrition profile, or hardware are confirmed. Any formula or safety-related claims would require appropriate expert review and testing before use.

Through 2027

Make sustainable work more supportive

If the project grows to employ full-time staff, our proposed retention policy is to offer five designated paid mental-health days per year and flexible scheduling through the end of 2027. Sorbeez has no full-time staff or such policy in place today.

↗

economic

Build a resilient business

Target Q4 2027

Look closer to home for partners

By Q4 2027, we aim to reduce shipping and logistics costs by selecting packaging and manufacturing suppliers within a regional radius, where feasible. Supplier availability, product requirements, and a real operating budget will determine whether this goal is achievable.

Target Q3 2027

Plan for steady, sustainable finances

Our proposed business milestone is positive monthly operating cash flow across distribution channels and recovery of new-equipment capital investment by Q3 2027. These are planning goals only; Sorbeez is not currently operating distribution channels or reporting operating cash flow.

✦

We’ll keep learning. Before any product is made, goals involving ingredients, packaging, safety, and sourcing need real suppliers, appropriate testing, and careful review. Until then, they remain part of our learning roadmap.
