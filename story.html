the scoop behind the scoops

A big idea, made together.

Meet the students behind Sorbeez and the classroom collaboration that brought this joyful food-business idea to life.

🍓🥭

from our classroom to your imagination

“What started in a high school classroom has evolved into a unified business mission to bring smiles and health to families everywhere.”

We are student business entrepreneurs from Redondo Union High School. Every single one of us was specially selected for this exclusive class. Through collaborative brainstorming, open discussions, and building on every comment, we created Sorbeez.

We saw an opportunity to reimagine the frozen sorbet industry with something that feels like a treat and is inspired by conversations about gut health. What began as a shared idea became a team effort in branding, product concepts, design, finance, and more.

Sorbeez is an educational business project—not a commercially available or medically tested food. We’re learning how thoughtful ideas grow while keeping our focus on joyful experiences for families.
