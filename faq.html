Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers about what Sorbeez is—and what we’re still figuring out.

Is Sorbeez dairy-free?

Sorbeez is currently a student product concept, and a final recipe has not been developed. We can’t confirm whether a finished product would be dairy-free; always check a verified ingredient and allergen label before consuming a food.

What is the idea behind probiotics?

Probiotics are one idea the student team is learning about. Sorbeez has no final recipe, and no ingredients or benefits have been verified. It is not a treatment or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Where are you located?

We’re a student entrepreneur team at Redondo Union High School in Redondo Beach, California.

Can I buy Sorbeez today?

Not yet. The flavor cards and cart are a website demo for our student project; no products are for sale and no orders or payments are processed.

What flavors are planned?

Our current flavor concepts are Mango Sunshine, Strawberry Smile, Blueberry Bounce, Peachy Keen, Tropical Twist, and Raspberry Rocket. Ingredients and nutrition facts remain under development.

Does the cart place an order?

No. The cart is only a demo counter stored in your browser to show how the interface could work. It does not reserve a product, send your information, or make a purchase.

For the grown-ups

Parents’ Corner

Sorbeez is a student product concept and is not available for purchase. No final product recipe has been developed. Ingredients, sugar content, allergens, vitamin amounts, and nutrition facts are not yet established or verified.

References to probiotics or digestion describe ideas the team is exploring—not proven benefits or medical claims. The Flavor Maker is pretend play; its decorative sprinkles do not represent real ingredients or supplements.

Before any future product could be offered, its formulation, safety, allergen information, and nutrition labeling would need appropriate review and verification.
