Curious about Sorbeez?

Here are a few helpful notes about our student business idea and what this website demo can—and can’t—do.

What is Sorbeez?
Sorbeez is a student-created fruit sorbet business concept from Redondo Union High School. The flavors and shopping bag on this site are concept demonstrations; the product is not currently for sale.
Is Sorbeez dairy-free?
We can’t confirm that yet. Recipes have not been finalized, and no allergen testing or manufacturing review has been completed. Please don’t rely on a concept page to assess food allergies or dietary needs.
How does Sorbeez aid kids’ digestion?
Digestive health is part of the idea behind Sorbeez, but no finished recipe, probiotic amount, fiber amount, digestive-enzyme activity, or health benefit has been verified. Sorbeez is not a treatment for digestive conditions. We would need appropriate formulation, testing, and expert review before making nutrition or health claims.
Does every flavor have multivitamins or probiotics?
Those are inspirations for the concept, not confirmed product ingredients. We don’t currently have a validated nutrition panel or confirmed probiotic or vitamin amounts for any flavor.
Where are you located?
Sorbeez is a student business project at Redondo Union High School in Redondo Beach, California. This page is not a retail or business mailing address.
Where can I buy Sorbeez?
You can’t purchase Sorbeez through this site. It is an educational project, not a live store, and the cart only saves flavor selections in your browser for demonstration.
Does the shopping bag place an order?
No. The bag counter is a small local demo, with no checkout, payment processing, or order fulfillment. Your selection stays in this browser’s local storage until you clear it.
How can I support the student team?
Visit the Sponsors page to learn about the project and email the team about sponsorship. This site is an educational project, not a live store.
